Privacy Policy — KotaHub Fitbit Trial

Last updated: November 13, 2026

Overview

KotaHub Fitbit Trial ("the App") is a personal application operated by a single individual ("the Owner") for the Owner's private use only. The App is not a public service and has no users other than the Owner.

Data accessed

With the Owner's consent, the App accesses the Owner's own Fitbit data through the Google Health API, using read-only scopes only:

  • Activity and fitness: steps and related activity summaries
  • Sleep: sleep duration and stages
  • Health metrics: resting heart rate, heart rate variability, blood oxygen saturation (SpO2), and weight

The App cannot modify or delete data in the Owner's Google or Fitbit accounts.

How data is used and stored

  • Data is retrieved solely for the Owner's personal health tracking and is stored in a private database on the Owner's own computer environment.
  • Data is never sold, rented, shared with, or disclosed to any third party.
  • Data is not used for advertising, marketing, or profiling.
  • OAuth credentials are stored locally with restricted file permissions and are never published.

Data deletion

The Owner may delete all stored data at any time by removing the local database, and may revoke the App's access at any time from the Google Account's third-party access settings, which immediately stops all further data retrieval.
